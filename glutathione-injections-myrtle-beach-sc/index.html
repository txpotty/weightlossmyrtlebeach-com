    How Glutathione Works in Myrtle Beach

    Glutathione is made from glutamate, cysteine and glycine. The liver produces the most, and the molecule neutralizes free radicals and regenerates vitamins C and E so they can keep working. Detox pathways depend on it. Aging, illness and long-term stress can lower glutathione, and an injection gets around the digestive breakdown that makes swallowed capsules unreliable.

    Who Glutathione Injections Are Usually For

    Adults interested in antioxidant support, recovery and general wellness are the usual candidates. People on GLP-1 medication or NAD+ sometimes add glutathione, but it has no effect on body weight and is not a treatment for a diagnosed disease. Skin lightening is excluded. People who are pregnant, breastfeeding, allergic or have unstable asthma are generally not candidates.

    Glutathione Dosing and Schedule in Myrtle Beach

    One to three injections a week. A common range is a few hundred milligrams per dose, injected into muscle or subcutaneously, with the route chosen by your provider. Refrigerate and protect from light. You will receive syringes, alcohol pads and written instructions for drawing up and injecting each dose.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. Noticeable changes are uncommon early on, and some patients report more energy or better workout recovery after two to four weeks while others notice little. Glutathione supports processes you cannot feel. Your provider checks in to review tolerance and adjust the plan.

    Glutathione Side Effects in Myrtle Beach

    Injection site pain leads the list. Some people get bruising or redness at the site, and a few notice nausea, cramps, bloating or a headache. Allergic reactions like rash or itching are uncommon but possible, and inhaled glutathione has caused bronchospasm in people with asthma, so providers take extra care with anyone who has breathing problems. Trouble breathing means call 911.

    Eligibility and Screening

    A provider reviews your history, medications and breathing health first. You will be asked about pregnancy, asthma, allergies, liver and kidney health and any chemotherapy or radiation, since glutathione may interact with some cancer therapies. Some cases get a metabolic panel. Upload recent labs if available.
