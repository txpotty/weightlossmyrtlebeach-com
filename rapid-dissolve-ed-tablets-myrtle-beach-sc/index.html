    Side Effects in Myrtle Beach

    Side effects follow the active ingredient. Sildenafil and tadalafil formulas both commonly cause headache, flushing, congestion and indigestion, with tadalafil adding back aches for some men.     Nitrates rule these tablets out, including nitroglycerin, isosorbide and poppers. Riociguat is also excluded.
