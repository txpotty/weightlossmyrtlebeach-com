    How Sermorelin Therapy Works in Myrtle Beach, SC

    How Sermorelin Works in Myrtle Beach

    Sermorelin is a 29 amino acid fragment of growth hormone releasing hormone. After injection it attaches to the receptors your own releasing hormone uses, which tells the pituitary to send out growth hormone in normal nightly pulses. Growth hormone then raises IGF-1. The body's own feedback system still applies, so growth hormone rarely overshoots the way it can with synthetic hormone injections. The original brand, Geref, was discontinued in 2008, and today's sermorelin is compounded.

    Who Sermorelin Is Usually For

    Typical candidates are adults over 35 who notice poorer sleep, slower recovery or a softer middle despite steady habits. Some have an IGF-1 that sits at the low end for their age, which gives the provider something concrete to track. It is not for weight loss alone. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, active cancer or a known pituitary tumor rules it out.

    Sermorelin Dosing and Schedule in Myrtle Beach

    Bedtime is the usual slot. Many compounded protocols start around 200 to 300 micrograms under the skin nightly, five to seven nights a week, though your provider sets your exact amount. Skipping food for two hours before the shot may help the pulse work as intended. Most shipments are a freeze-dried powder that you reconstitute with bacteriostatic water and then refrigerate.

    What the First Three Months Look Like

    Weeks one and two are mostly about the routine. Deeper sleep is the earliest change some patients report, though many feel no difference yet. Around weeks four to twelve, energy and workout recovery may improve, and body composition changes, if any, come slowly after that. Your provider often rechecks IGF-1 around week six to eight.

    Sermorelin Side Effects in Myrtle Beach

    Redness, itching or a small bump where you inject is the most common complaint. In the first weeks some people get facial flushing, a headache or brief dizziness after the shot. Less often, people report trouble falling asleep, restlessness or a changed sense of taste, and any hives or trouble breathing needs emergency care. Tell your provider at the next check-in.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Before any prescription, your provider reads the intake for cancer history, pituitary problems, thyroid disease, diabetes, sleep apnea and pregnancy plans. Baseline labs often include IGF-1, a metabolic panel and TSH. Untreated hypothyroidism can blunt the response, and glucocorticoids such as prednisone can interfere with it, so list every medication. Lab results can be uploaded.

    Sermorelin Compared to Other Peptides in Myrtle Beach

    People ask about CJC-1295 most. The CJC-1295 and ipamorelin combination works on the same axis with a longer action and a second receptor target. A related releasing hormone analog, tesamorelin, carries FDA approval as Egrifta SV for HIV-associated abdominal fat, which is a narrow indication. Actual growth hormone, by contrast, is a different drug for adults with confirmed deficiency.
